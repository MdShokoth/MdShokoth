## Research

### Cutting-temperature prediction under hybrid nanofluid-based MQL

**Undergraduate team thesis · BUET · 2023–2024**  
*Prediction of Cutting Temperature in Turning Medium Carbon Steel under Hybrid Nanofluid-Based MQL*

| Experimental work | Computational work |
| :--- | :--- |
| Turning medium-carbon steel under minimum quantity lubrication | Predicting cutting temperature from machining parameters and nanofluid concentration |
| ZnO–MoS₂ hybrid nanoparticles in coconut oil | Comparing artificial neural networks, gradient boosting, and XGBoost |
| Four concentrations: 0%, 0.2%, 0.6%, and 1% | Evaluating predictions against experimental measurements |
| 64 experimental combinations; temperature and chip measurements | Interpreting model behavior alongside physical process trends |

**Hands-on experience:** nanofluid preparation, tool–work thermocouple calibration, turning experiments, and chip-thickness measurement.

**Research takeaway:** the effect of nanofluid concentration depended on the machining conditions. This strengthened my interest in models that remain useful when process conditions change.
