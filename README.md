### About me

I am an Industrial and Production Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with a CGPA of **3.86/4.00**, ranking **3rd among 50 students**. I have teaching experience at Daffodil International University and Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology.
